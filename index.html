05 / The Journey

FROM THE UNDERGROUND TO THE PEOPLE.

Syko Kyan has been active since 2014, rooted in Sri Lankan street hip-hop culture. His music moves between melodic drill, boom-bap and Sinhala storytelling.

His work carries stories about hardship, family loyalty, street reality and personal struggle. The Bandanagare series became a defining part of his catalog, while PARADOX expanded the scope through a wide network of collaborators.

GOD GANG and Kyan Family represent the collective side of the movement, connecting music, visuals and creative direction.
